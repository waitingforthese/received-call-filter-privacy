1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may process the following information:

  • Your Google account email address used for authentication.
  • Your name and mobile number provided during registration.
  • Information relating to your application account and registration status.
  • Contact names and phone numbers that you add to your Allowed Contacts list.
  • Call-related information required for the call filtering functionality.
  • Information required to provide the application's SMS functionality.
  • Application settings and usage statistics required for the application's features.

2. Google Account Sign-In

RECEIVED CALL FILTER uses Google Sign-In for account authentication. The application uses your Google account identity to identify and authenticate your account.

The application does not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail, Google Photos, or other Google account content unless such access is separately requested and clearly disclosed in the future.

3. Contacts Information

The application may access contact information when required for its contact-management and call-filtering functionality.

Contacts that you add to the application's Allowed Contacts list may include the contact name and telephone number. This information is used to determine which incoming calls are allowed according to your selected settings.

4. Call Information

RECEIVED CALL FILTER uses Android call-screening functionality to identify incoming calls and apply the filtering settings configured by the user.

When call protection is enabled, calls from numbers that are not included in the user's Allowed Contacts list may be rejected according to the application's configured filtering behavior.

The application may process information about incoming and outgoing calls when required to provide its call filtering and call-end functionality.

5. SMS Functionality

The application may use SMS functionality to send an automated message according to the settings enabled by the user.

SMS messages are used only for the application's disclosed call-related features, such as sending an automated response after a rejected or completed call when the corresponding feature is enabled.

The user is responsible for reviewing and enabling SMS-related settings and for any applicable mobile-network or SMS charges.

6. Cloud Storage and Firebase

The application may use Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to provide account authentication and cloud-based application functionality.

Information such as account details, registration information, application settings, and Allowed Contacts information may be stored in the cloud when required to provide the application's services.

Access to cloud-stored information is controlled through authentication and database security rules.

7. How We Use Information

Information processed by the application may be used to:

  • Authenticate and manage user accounts.
  • Process customer registrations.
  • Maintain the user's Allowed Contacts list.
  • Provide call filtering functionality.
  • Provide call-related SMS functionality.
  • Synchronize relevant application information with cloud services.
  • Maintain application settings and statistics.
  • Provide technical support and troubleshoot application issues.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the application.

8. Data Sharing

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.
